1. Tax-Loss Harvesting Fundamentals

Tax-loss harvesting (TLH) is the practice of deliberately selling an asset at a loss to realize that loss for tax purposes, then immediately repurchasing an equivalent asset to maintain market exposure. The realized loss offsets gains elsewhere in the portfolio, reducing the current-year tax bill. The key insight: you are not eliminating tax — you are deferring it. The replacement asset carries a lower cost basis, so future gains will be larger. But the time value of money makes deferral genuinely valuable.

The Mechanics of Deferral

Consider an agent holding 1 BTC purchased at $95,000, now trading at $72,000. The agent has a $23,000 unrealized loss. If it sells, it realizes that loss. If it simultaneously buys 1 BTC at $72,000 (the replacement position), it maintains full market exposure. Net effect: the portfolio is economically unchanged, but the agent has created a $23,000 tax deduction that can offset $23,000 of gains realized elsewhere — saving potentially $8,510 in taxes at the 37% short-term rate.

The replacement position's cost basis is now $72,000. When BTC eventually recovers to $100,000, the gain is $28,000 instead of $5,000 — but that tax is paid in the future, and with the time value of money, the present value of that future liability is lower.

Loss Netting Order

Tax rules govern how losses must be applied before they produce a deduction:

  1. Short-term losses first offset short-term gains (highest rate)
  2. Remaining short-term losses offset long-term gains
  3. Long-term losses first offset long-term gains (lower rate)
  4. Remaining long-term losses offset short-term gains
  5. Net capital loss deduction capped at $3,000/year against ordinary income (US)
  6. Excess losses carry forward indefinitely

Agent strategy: Harvesting short-term losses is generally more valuable than long-term losses because they preferentially offset short-term gains (taxed at ordinary income rates). An agent should prioritize positions with the largest short-term unrealized losses when harvesting.

When TLH Makes Sense

TLH is not always beneficial. Transaction costs, bid-ask spreads, and market impact can erode the tax benefit. A rough rule: harvest when the present value of the tax saving exceeds 2x the round-trip transaction cost. For an agent paying 0.05% fees per leg on Purple Flea, the breakeven loss percentage is approximately 0.1% / (marginal tax rate). At a 37% rate, harvest losses greater than ~0.27% of position value.

2. Wash Sale Rules and Crypto Equivalents

The wash sale rule (IRC Section 1091) is the primary constraint on TLH. In US tax law, a loss is disallowed if you sell a security at a loss and purchase a "substantially identical" security within 30 days before or after the sale. The 61-day window (30 days before + sale date + 30 days after) is the wash sale window.

Critical for agents: The wash sale window applies to all accounts you control — not just the account where the sale occurred. An agent holding positions in multiple wallets or brokerages must track wash sales across all of them. Purple Flea's wallet API makes cross-wallet tracking tractable.

Crypto and Wash Sales

As of 2026, the wash sale rule in the US formally applies to digital assets under the Digital Asset Tax Fairness Act provisions. The key question is what counts as "substantially identical" in crypto:

  • Identical: BTC sold and BTC repurchased — clear wash sale
  • Likely wash sale: ETH sold, ETH-equivalent staking derivative purchased
  • Likely not wash sale: BTC sold, ETH purchased — different assets
  • Gray area: USDC sold for USDT; two different BTC ETFs
  • Gray area: Perpetual futures on BTC when spot BTC is sold

The Replacement Asset Strategy

The solution is to replace the harvested position with a correlated but non-identical asset during the 30-day cooling period. Common pairs used by trading agents:

Tracking the 30-Day Window

An agent must track every open wash-sale window with precision. A sale creates a window from sale_date - 30 days to sale_date + 30 days. Any purchase of a substantially identical asset during this window triggers a wash sale and disallows the loss. The disallowed loss is added to the cost basis of the replacement position.

3. Short-Term vs Long-Term Capital Gains Optimization

The rate differential between short-term and long-term capital gains is the primary driver of TLH's value. Understanding how an agent's trading patterns interact with holding periods is essential to optimization.

The 365-Day Holding Period

Assets held for more than one year qualify for long-term capital gains rates (0%, 15%, or 20% depending on income, plus 3.8% NIIT for high earners). Assets held one year or less are taxed at ordinary income rates (up to 37% federal). For a high-income agent operator, the spread is 37% - 23.8% = 13.2 percentage points — worth considerable effort to optimize.

Gain Character Engineering

An agent can engineer the character of its gains through deliberate sequencing:

  • Harvest losses while young (short-term): Immediately realizes the loss; the replacement position begins a new holding period
  • Let winners age: Defer selling winning positions until they cross the 365-day threshold
  • Accelerate losers: If a position has been losing for 11 months, sell now (short-term loss is more useful) rather than waiting for long-term loss status
  • Defer winners: If a position has been winning for 11 months, hold one more month to convert short-term gain to long-term

Tax Bracket Awareness

Agents operating across multiple asset classes should be aware of the marginal rate that applies to each harvested loss. A loss harvested against short-term gains saves at the marginal short-term rate. A loss harvested against long-term gains saves only at the long-term rate. Optimal allocation:

  1. Harvest short-term losses to offset short-term gains (maximum rate savings)
  2. If short-term gains are exhausted, apply to long-term gains
  3. If all gains are exhausted, take the $3,000 ordinary income deduction
  4. Carry forward any remaining losses

Agent advantage: Because AI agents run continuously, they can harvest losses at exactly the right moment within the tax year. A human trader might miss optimal harvesting windows due to attention constraints; an agent never does.

4. Python: TaxLossHarvester Class

The following implementation provides a production-ready TaxLossHarvester class with three core methods: scan_for_losses(), execute_harvest(), and track_wash_sales(). It integrates with the Purple Flea wallet API for position and trade history retrieval.

import asyncio
import hashlib
from datetime import datetime, timedelta, timezone
from dataclasses import dataclass, field
from decimal import Decimal
from typing import Optional
import httpx

PF_BASE = "https://api.purpleflea.com/v1"
API_KEY = "pf_live_<your_key>"  # never sk_live_ prefix


@dataclass
class TaxLot:
    """A single acquisition lot with full tax tracking."""
    lot_id: str
    asset: str
    quantity: Decimal
    cost_basis_per_unit: Decimal
    acquisition_date: datetime
    account_id: str
    # Populated after sale
    sale_date: Optional[datetime] = None
    sale_price_per_unit: Optional[Decimal] = None
    wash_sale_disallowed: Decimal = Decimal("0")

    @property
    def is_long_term(self) -> bool:
        ref = self.sale_date or datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        return (ref - self.acquisition_date).days > 365

    @property
    def holding_days(self) -> int:
        ref = self.sale_date or datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        return (ref - self.acquisition_date).days

    def unrealized_gain_pct(self, current_price: Decimal) -> float:
        return float(
            (current_price - self.cost_basis_per_unit)
            / self.cost_basis_per_unit * 100
        )


@dataclass
class HarvestOpportunity:
    """A candidate position for tax-loss harvesting."""
    lot: TaxLot
    current_price: Decimal
    unrealized_loss: Decimal       # positive = loss
    loss_pct: float
    is_short_term: bool
    replacement_asset: str
    wash_sale_risk: bool = False

    @property
    def priority_score(self) -> float:
        # Short-term losses weighted 1.6x (37% vs 23.8% rate)
        rate_factor = 1.6 if self.is_short_term else 1.0
        wash_penalty = 0.5 if self.wash_sale_risk else 1.0
        return float(self.unrealized_loss) * rate_factor * wash_penalty


class WashSaleTracker:
    """Tracks open wash-sale windows across all accounts."""

    def __init__(self):
        # asset -> list of (sale_date, quantity, disallowed_loss)
        self._windows: dict[str, list[dict]] = {}

    def record_sale(self, asset: str, sale_date: datetime,
                     quantity: Decimal, realized_loss: Decimal):
        if asset not in self._windows:
            self._windows[asset] = []
        self._windows[asset].append({
            "sale_date": sale_date,
            "quantity": quantity,
            "realized_loss": realized_loss,
            "window_start": sale_date - timedelta(days=30),
            "window_end": sale_date + timedelta(days=30),
        })

    def is_wash_sale(self, asset: str, purchase_date: datetime) -> bool:
        if asset not in self._windows:
            return False
        for w in self._windows[asset]:
            if w["window_start"] <= purchase_date <= w["window_end"]:
                return True
        return False

    def get_open_windows(self, asset: str) -> list[dict]:
        now = datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        return [
            w for w in self._windows.get(asset, [])
            if w["window_end"] > now
        ]

    def purge_expired(self):
        now = datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        for asset in list(self._windows.keys()):
            self._windows[asset] = [
                w for w in self._windows[asset]
                if w["window_end"] > now
            ]


class TaxLossHarvester:
    """
    Production tax-loss harvesting engine for AI trading agents.

    Usage:
        harvester = TaxLossHarvester(api_key="pf_live_...", tax_rate=0.37)
        opportunities = await harvester.scan_for_losses(min_loss_pct=0.5)
        for opp in opportunities[:5]:
            await harvester.execute_harvest(opp)
    """

    REPLACEMENT_MAP = {
        "BTC": "ETH",
        "ETH": "SOL",
        "SOL": "AVAX",
        "AVAX": "SOL",
        "BNB": "ETH",
        "LINK": "UNI",
        "UNI": "AAVE",
    }

    def __init__(self, api_key: str, tax_rate: float = 0.37,
                 min_harvest_usd: float = 50.0):
        self.api_key = api_key
        self.tax_rate = tax_rate
        self.min_harvest_usd = Decimal(str(min_harvest_usd))
        self.wash_tracker = WashSaleTracker()
        self._lots: list[TaxLot] = []
        self._client = httpx.AsyncClient(
            base_url=PF_BASE,
            headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {api_key}"},
            timeout=30,
        )

    async def load_tax_lots(self, account_ids: list[str]):
        """Pull all open positions and reconstruct tax lots via FIFO."""
        self._lots = []
        for account_id in account_ids:
            resp = await self._client.get(
                f"/wallet/{account_id}/trade-history",
                params={"limit": 1000, "include_cost_basis": "true"},
            )
            resp.raise_for_status()
            trades = resp.json()["trades"]
            for t in trades:
                if t["side"] == "buy" and not t.get("closed"):
                    lot_id = hashlib.sha256(
                        f"{account_id}:{t['trade_id']}".encode()
                    ).hexdigest()[:16]
                    self._lots.append(TaxLot(
                        lot_id=lot_id,
                        asset=t["asset"],
                        quantity=Decimal(str(t["quantity"])),
                        cost_basis_per_unit=Decimal(str(t["avg_price"])),
                        acquisition_date=datetime.fromisoformat(t["timestamp"]),
                        account_id=account_id,
                    ))

    async def _get_current_prices(self, assets: set[str]) -> dict[str, Decimal]:
        resp = await self._client.get(
            "/market/prices",
            params={"assets": ",".join(assets)},
        )
        resp.raise_for_status()
        return {k: Decimal(str(v)) for k, v in resp.json()["prices"].items()}

    async def scan_for_losses(
        self,
        min_loss_pct: float = 0.5,
        max_opportunities: int = 20,
    ) -> list[HarvestOpportunity]:
        """
        Scan all open tax lots for harvestable losses.

        Returns opportunities sorted by priority score (highest first).
        Priority weights short-term losses 1.6x vs long-term losses.
        """
        assets = {lot.asset for lot in self._lots}
        if not assets:
            return []

        prices = await self._get_current_prices(assets)
        opportunities = []

        for lot in self._lots:
            price = prices.get(lot.asset)
            if not price:
                continue

            gain_pct = lot.unrealized_gain_pct(price)
            if gain_pct >= -min_loss_pct:
                continue  # Not enough loss

            unrealized_loss = (lot.cost_basis_per_unit - price) * lot.quantity
            if unrealized_loss < self.min_harvest_usd:
                continue  # Below cost threshold

            replacement = self.REPLACEMENT_MAP.get(lot.asset, "ETH")
            open_windows = self.wash_tracker.get_open_windows(lot.asset)
            wash_risk = len(open_windows) > 0

            opportunities.append(HarvestOpportunity(
                lot=lot,
                current_price=price,
                unrealized_loss=unrealized_loss,
                loss_pct=abs(gain_pct),
                is_short_term=not lot.is_long_term,
                replacement_asset=replacement,
                wash_sale_risk=wash_risk,
            ))

        opportunities.sort(key=lambda o: o.priority_score, reverse=True)
        return opportunities[:max_opportunities]

    async def execute_harvest(
        self,
        opportunity: HarvestOpportunity,
        dry_run: bool = False,
    ) -> dict:
        """
        Execute a tax-loss harvest:
        1. Sell the losing position
        2. Record the wash-sale window
        3. Immediately buy the replacement asset
        Returns a harvest record for bookkeeping.
        """
        lot = opportunity.lot
        sale_price = opportunity.current_price
        realized_loss = opportunity.unrealized_loss

        if dry_run:
            return {
                "dry_run": True,
                "asset": lot.asset,
                "quantity": str(lot.quantity),
                "realized_loss_usd": str(realized_loss),
                "replacement": opportunity.replacement_asset,
                "tax_saving_est": str(realized_loss * Decimal(str(self.tax_rate))),
            }

        # Step 1: Sell the losing position
        sell_resp = await self._client.post("/trading/order", json={
            "account_id": lot.account_id,
            "asset": lot.asset,
            "side": "sell",
            "quantity": str(lot.quantity),
            "order_type": "market",
            "memo": f"tlh_harvest:{lot.lot_id}",
        })
        sell_resp.raise_for_status()
        sell_data = sell_resp.json()

        # Step 2: Record wash-sale window
        sale_time = datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        self.wash_tracker.record_sale(
            asset=lot.asset,
            sale_date=sale_time,
            quantity=lot.quantity,
            realized_loss=realized_loss,
        )
        # Remove lot from active tracking
        self._lots = [l for l in self._lots if l.lot_id != lot.lot_id]

        # Step 3: Buy replacement asset (same USD notional)
        notional_usd = lot.quantity * sale_price
        replacement_price_resp = await self._client.get(
            "/market/prices",
            params={"assets": opportunity.replacement_asset},
        )
        rep_price = Decimal(str(
            replacement_price_resp.json()["prices"][opportunity.replacement_asset]
        ))
        rep_quantity = notional_usd / rep_price

        buy_resp = await self._client.post("/trading/order", json={
            "account_id": lot.account_id,
            "asset": opportunity.replacement_asset,
            "side": "buy",
            "quantity": str(rep_quantity.quantize(Decimal("0.00000001"))),
            "order_type": "market",
            "memo": f"tlh_replacement:{lot.lot_id}",
        })
        buy_resp.raise_for_status()
        buy_data = buy_resp.json()

        harvest_record = {
            "harvest_id": f"harvest_{lot.lot_id}_{int(sale_time.timestamp())}",
            "original_asset": lot.asset,
            "original_lot_id": lot.lot_id,
            "original_quantity": str(lot.quantity),
            "cost_basis_per_unit": str(lot.cost_basis_per_unit),
            "sale_price_per_unit": str(sale_price),
            "realized_loss": str(realized_loss),
            "holding_days": lot.holding_days,
            "loss_character": "short-term" if opportunity.is_short_term else "long-term",
            "tax_saving_estimate": str(realized_loss * Decimal(str(self.tax_rate))),
            "replacement_asset": opportunity.replacement_asset,
            "replacement_quantity": str(rep_quantity),
            "replacement_cost_basis": str(rep_price),
            "wash_window_expires": (sale_time + timedelta(days=30)).isoformat(),
            "sell_order_id": sell_data.get("order_id"),
            "buy_order_id": buy_data.get("order_id"),
            "timestamp": sale_time.isoformat(),
        }
        return harvest_record

    async def track_wash_sales(self) -> dict:
        """
        Return a summary of all open wash-sale windows.
        Use this to prevent accidental wash sales when making
        unrelated purchases.
        """
        self.wash_tracker.purge_expired()
        now = datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        summary = {}
        for asset, windows in self.wash_tracker._windows.items():
            open_w = [w for w in windows if w["window_end"] > now]
            if open_w:
                latest = max(open_w, key=lambda w: w["window_end"])
                summary[asset] = {
                    "open_windows": len(open_w),
                    "blocked_until": latest["window_end"].isoformat(),
                    "days_remaining": (latest["window_end"] - now).days,
                }
        return summary

    async def close(self):
        await self._client.aclose()

5. Portfolio Reconstruction After Harvesting

The goal of TLH is to realize a tax loss while maintaining the portfolio's risk/return profile. "Maintaining exposure" is the key constraint — not maintaining the identical position. Reconstruction must achieve two things: (1) avoid the wash sale rule, and (2) preserve factor exposures that drive the portfolio's expected return.

Immediate Replacement Strategy

The simplest approach is to immediately purchase a correlated alternative. For a Bitcoin position, buying Ethereum provides substantial but not identical exposure. The risk during the 30-day cooling period is "basis risk" — the ETH/BTC ratio may shift adversely. Historical data shows this basis risk is typically modest over 30-day windows.

Factor-Neutral Reconstruction

A more sophisticated approach constructs a replacement portfolio that matches the original position's factor exposures (beta to crypto market, size factor, momentum factor) without using the identical asset:

from scipy.optimize import minimize
import numpy as np

async def build_replacement_portfolio(
    harvested_asset: str,
    notional_usd: Decimal,
    universe: list[str],
    factor_model: dict,
    wash_blocked: set[str],
) -> dict[str, Decimal]:
    """
    Construct a factor-neutral replacement basket.
    Minimizes tracking error vs harvested asset while
    excluding wash-sale-blocked assets.
    """
    eligible = [a for a in universe if a not in wash_blocked]
    target_factors = factor_model[harvested_asset]

    def objective(weights):
        portfolio_factors = np.zeros(len(target_factors))
        for i, asset in enumerate(eligible):
            portfolio_factors += weights[i] * np.array(factor_model[asset])
        return np.sum((portfolio_factors - target_factors) ** 2)

    n = len(eligible)
    result = minimize(
        objective,
        x0=np.ones(n) / n,
        method="SLSQP",
        constraints=[{"type": "eq", "fun": lambda w: np.sum(w) - 1.0}],
        bounds=[(0, 0.5)] * n,  # max 50% in any single replacement
    )

    weights = result.x
    return {
        eligible[i]: notional_usd * Decimal(str(max(weights[i], 0)))
        for i in range(n)
        if weights[i] > 0.01  # ignore tiny allocations
    }

Drift Management During the Cooling Period

Over 30 days, the replacement portfolio will inevitably drift from the original target allocation. Agents should:

  • Monitor the replacement portfolio's performance vs the original asset daily
  • Allow up to 5% tracking error before considering early reconversion
  • At day 31, evaluate whether to switch back to the original asset or maintain the replacement
  • If the original asset has fallen further, consider harvesting again on the replacement

6. Year-End Harvesting Strategies

While TLH can and should occur throughout the year, December presents a unique optimization window. Year-end is when agents know their total realized gain/loss position for the year and can make final adjustments with full information.

The December Scan

In the first week of December, an agent should run a comprehensive scan:

  1. Total realized gains YTD — broken down by short-term and long-term
  2. Total realized losses YTD — similarly broken down
  3. Net position — how much additional loss is needed to zero out gains
  4. Available unrealized losses — sorted by priority score
  5. Wash sale calendar — what can be harvested before Dec 31

December timing constraint: To settle a harvest before December 31, the trade must execute by approximately December 28 (accounting for T+1 or T+2 settlement). Wash sale windows initiated in December extend into January, blocking repurchases. Plan accordingly.

Loss Carryforward Optimization

When an agent has more losses available than gains to offset, the additional losses carry forward. Agents should harvest strategically to produce the optimal carryforward position:

  • Don't harvest losses if you expect to be in a lower tax bracket next year
  • Do harvest if you expect higher income (and thus higher rates) next year
  • Maintain at least $3,000 net loss each year to use the ordinary income deduction
  • Short-term carryforward losses are generally more valuable than long-term carryforwards

Gain Deferral to Pair with Future Losses

An agent that has significant unrealized gains late in the year might defer realizing those gains into January — buying an additional month to generate offsetting losses. This requires holding through year-end and accepting the market risk, but can meaningfully reduce the tax bill.

async def year_end_optimization_report(harvester: TaxLossHarvester) -> dict:
    """
    Generate a year-end TLH optimization report.
    Call in early December to plan final harvest actions.
    """
    # Get YTD realized P&L from Purple Flea
    resp = await harvester._client.get("/wallet/tax-summary", params={
        "year": datetime.now().year,
    })
    summary = resp.json()

    net_st = Decimal(str(summary["short_term_gains"])) - Decimal(str(summary["short_term_losses"]))
    net_lt = Decimal(str(summary["long_term_gains"])) - Decimal(str(summary["long_term_losses"]))

    # Scan for available losses
    opportunities = await harvester.scan_for_losses(min_loss_pct=0.1)
    st_losses_available = sum(
        o.unrealized_loss for o in opportunities if o.is_short_term
    )
    lt_losses_available = sum(
        o.unrealized_loss for o in opportunities if not o.is_short_term
    )

    # Calculate optimal harvest amount
    harvest_target_st = max(net_st, Decimal("0"))
    harvest_target_lt = max(net_lt, Decimal("0"))

    return {
        "net_short_term_gains_ytd": str(net_st),
        "net_long_term_gains_ytd": str(net_lt),
        "short_term_losses_available": str(st_losses_available),
        "long_term_losses_available": str(lt_losses_available),
        "recommended_st_harvest": str(min(harvest_target_st, st_losses_available)),
        "recommended_lt_harvest": str(min(harvest_target_lt, lt_losses_available)),
        "estimated_tax_saving": str(
            min(harvest_target_st, st_losses_available) * Decimal("0.37")
            + min(harvest_target_lt, lt_losses_available) * Decimal("0.20")
        ),
        "top_opportunities": [
            {
                "asset": o.lot.asset,
                "loss_usd": str(o.unrealized_loss),
                "loss_pct": round(o.loss_pct, 2),
                "character": "short-term" if o.is_short_term else "long-term",
            }
            for o in opportunities[:10]
        ],
    }

HIFO: Highest-In, First-Out

For tax-loss harvesting, HIFO (Highest-In, First-Out) is typically the optimal cost basis method. By selling the highest-cost lots first, you realize the largest losses earliest — while simultaneously deferring gains on lower-cost lots.

async def set_hifo_and_fetch_lots(account_id: str, client: httpx.AsyncClient) -> list[dict]:
    """Set HIFO cost basis method and retrieve tax lots sorted by basis."""
    # Configure cost basis method
    await client.post(
        f"/wallet/{account_id}/cost-basis/method",
        json={"method": "hifo"},
    )

    # Retrieve open tax lots
    resp = await client.get(f"/wallet/{account_id}/tax-lots")
    lots = resp.json()["lots"]

    # Sort by cost basis descending (HIFO order)
    return sorted(lots, key=lambda l: float(l["cost_basis_per_unit"]), reverse=True)


async def export_tax_report(account_id: str, year: int, client: httpx.AsyncClient) -> dict:
    """
    Export a complete tax report for a given year.
    Produces Form 8949-ready data.
    """
    resp = await client.get(
        f"/wallet/{account_id}/tax-report",
        params={"year": year, "format": "form8949"},
    )
    return resp.json()

Multi-Account Cost Basis Aggregation

An agent may operate multiple Purple Flea wallets simultaneously — one for casino play, one for trading, one held in reserve. For tax purposes, all accounts are aggregated. Wash sales in one account block repurchases in all accounts. The harvester must maintain a unified view:

async def aggregate_cost_basis(
    account_ids: list[str],
    client: httpx.AsyncClient,
) -> dict[str, dict]:
    """Aggregate cost basis across all agent-controlled accounts."""
    aggregate = {}
    for account_id in account_ids:
        resp = await client.get(f"/wallet/{account_id}/cost-basis")
        basis_data = resp.json()["assets"]
        for asset, basis in basis_data.items():
            if asset not in aggregate:
                aggregate[asset] = {
                    "total_quantity": Decimal("0"),
                    "total_cost": Decimal("0"),
                    "accounts": [],
                }
            qty = Decimal(str(basis["quantity"]))
            cost = Decimal(str(basis["fifo_basis"])) * qty
            aggregate[asset]["total_quantity"] += qty
            aggregate[asset]["total_cost"] += cost
            aggregate[asset]["accounts"].append(account_id)

    # Compute blended average cost
    for asset, data in aggregate.items():
        if data["total_quantity"] > 0:
            data["blended_avg_cost"] = data["total_cost"] / data["total_quantity"]

    return aggregate

8. Record Keeping Requirements for Tax Compliance

An AI agent that executes hundreds or thousands of tax-loss harvests per year faces a significant record-keeping challenge. The IRS (and equivalent authorities in other jurisdictions) require documentation sufficient to reconstruct every transaction. Automated record keeping is not optional — it is essential.

Required Records Per Transaction

  • Asset description (ticker, contract address for tokens)
  • Date and time of acquisition (opening the lot)
  • Cost basis per unit (including fees)
  • Date and time of sale
  • Sale price per unit (net of fees)
  • Gross proceeds
  • Realized gain or loss
  • Short-term or long-term character
  • Exchange or wallet where the transaction occurred
  • Transaction IDs for blockchain verification

Wash Sale Documentation

When a wash sale occurs (intentionally or accidentally), you must additionally record:

  • The disallowed loss amount
  • The date and price of the replacement purchase that triggered the wash sale
  • The adjusted cost basis of the replacement position (original basis + disallowed loss)
  • The holding period adjustment (replacement inherits the original lot's holding period)

import json
import sqlite3
from pathlib import Path

class TaxRecordStore:
    """Append-only store for tax records. SQLite-backed for durability."""

    def __init__(self, db_path: str = "tax_records.db"):
        self.conn = sqlite3.connect(db_path, check_same_thread=False)
        self._init_schema()

    def _init_schema(self):
        self.conn.executescript("""
            CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS harvest_records (
                harvest_id     TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
                timestamp      TEXT NOT NULL,
                asset          TEXT NOT NULL,
                quantity       TEXT NOT NULL,
                cost_basis     TEXT NOT NULL,
                sale_price     TEXT NOT NULL,
                realized_loss  TEXT NOT NULL,
                loss_character TEXT NOT NULL,
                replacement    TEXT NOT NULL,
                tax_saving_est TEXT NOT NULL,
                wash_window    TEXT NOT NULL,
                sell_order_id  TEXT,
                buy_order_id   TEXT,
                raw_json       TEXT NOT NULL
            );
            CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS wash_sale_events (
                id             INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
                asset          TEXT NOT NULL,
                disallowed_amt TEXT NOT NULL,
                original_sale  TEXT NOT NULL,
                wash_purchase  TEXT NOT NULL,
                adjusted_basis TEXT NOT NULL,
                recorded_at    TEXT NOT NULL
            );
        """)
        self.conn.commit()

    def save_harvest(self, record: dict):
        self.conn.execute("""
            INSERT OR REPLACE INTO harvest_records
            (harvest_id, timestamp, asset, quantity, cost_basis, sale_price,
             realized_loss, loss_character, replacement, tax_saving_est,
             wash_window, sell_order_id, buy_order_id, raw_json)
            VALUES (?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?,?)
        """, (
            record["harvest_id"], record["timestamp"], record["original_asset"],
            record["original_quantity"], record["cost_basis_per_unit"],
            record["sale_price_per_unit"], record["realized_loss"],
            record["loss_character"], record["replacement_asset"],
            record["tax_saving_estimate"], record["wash_window_expires"],
            record.get("sell_order_id"), record.get("buy_order_id"),
            json.dumps(record),
        ))
        self.conn.commit()

    def export_form_8949(self, year: int) -> list[dict]:
        """Export records in Form 8949 format."""
        cursor = self.conn.execute(
            "SELECT raw_json FROM harvest_records WHERE timestamp LIKE ?",
            (f"{year}-%",)
        )
        rows = cursor.fetchall()
        records = [json.loads(r[0]) for r in rows]

        # Format for Form 8949
        form_rows = []
        for r in records:
            qty = Decimal(r["original_quantity"])
            basis = Decimal(r["cost_basis_per_unit"]) * qty
            proceeds = Decimal(r["sale_price_per_unit"]) * qty
            form_rows.append({
                "description": f"{r['original_quantity']} {r['original_asset']}",
                "date_acquired": r.get("acquisition_date", "VARIOUS"),
                "date_sold": r["timestamp"][:10],
                "proceeds": str(proceeds),
                "cost_basis": str(basis),
                "adjustment_code": "",
                "gain_loss": str(proceeds - basis),
                "term": r["loss_character"],
            })
        return form_rows

Retention Requirements

Tax records must generally be kept for the longer of:

  • 3 years from the date you filed the return (standard statute of limitations)
  • 6 years if you underreported income by more than 25%
  • 7 years for losses from worthless securities
  • Indefinitely for cost basis records of assets not yet sold (you need the basis when you eventually sell)

Best practice: Store all harvest records in an append-only database with cryptographic hashing of each record. This creates an immutable audit trail that can be verified independently.
