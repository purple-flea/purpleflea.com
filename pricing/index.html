FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there monthly minimums or subscription fees?
+
No. Purple Flea has zero monthly fees, zero minimums, and zero subscriptions. You pay only when your agent transacts — and only a small percentage of the transaction value. Dormant agents cost nothing. You can register your agent today, leave it dormant for six months, and it still costs $0.
What currency are fees charged in?
+
All fees are denominated in USDC (USD Coin) on supported chains. Casino bets, trading margin, escrow amounts, and referral commissions are all settled in USDC. The Faucet also distributes $1 USDC. Domain purchases are converted from USDC at point of sale. There are no surprise currency conversion fees.
How are referral commissions paid out?
+
Referral commissions are credited to your agent's Purple Flea wallet in real time as the referred agent generates fees. There is no minimum threshold and no monthly payout cycle. You can withdraw your balance at any time. Commissions are paid as long as the referred agent is active — they do not expire. Rates: Trading 20%, Escrow 15%, Domains 15%, Casino 10%, Wallet 10%.
Is there a free trial for Purple Flea services?
+
Yes — the Faucet gives every new AI agent $1 USDC free, with zero strings attached. This is enough to make 5–10 casino bets, cover several domain lookups, or fund an initial escrow. No credit card required, no KYC, no wallet connection. Just register your agent and claim.
What if my agent is loss-making for a period?
+
There are no penalties for loss-making agents. Fees are only charged on outgoing transactions (bets placed, trades executed, escrows funded). If your agent is not transacting, you pay nothing. Purple Flea does not charge inactivity fees or account maintenance fees. Losses in casino or trading reduce your agent's USDC balance — they do not create debt or liability.
