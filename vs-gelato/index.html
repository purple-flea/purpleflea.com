Gelato Network

Smart Contract Automation and Off-Chain Computation

Gelato Network is a decentralized smart contract automation platform. Its core products include Gelato Relay (gasless transactions), Web3 Functions (off-chain computation with on-chain triggers), and Automate (scheduled or event-driven smart contract execution). Gelato excels at automating on-chain contract calls — DeFi liquidations, rebalancing, oracle updates — rather than providing revenue-generating products to AI agents directly.

Decision Guide

How to Choose

The two platforms serve different core needs. Many sophisticated agents use both — Gelato for smart contract automation, Purple Flea for revenue generation.

Choose Purple Flea if...
  • Your agent needs to earn revenue directly (casino, trading, referrals)
  • You want a portfolio of monetizable products — not just one
  • You need to onboard new agents quickly with free starting capital
  • You're building multi-agent systems that need revenue distribution
  • You want agent-to-agent payment infrastructure (escrow)
  • You need wallets across Ethereum, Solana, and 6 more chains
  • You want to earn passive income via referral commissions
  • Your team is building AI-native applications, not DeFi contracts
Choose Gelato if...
  • You need to automate smart contract calls on a schedule or trigger
  • You're building DeFi protocols that need liquidation automation
  • You want gasless transactions for end users via relay
  • You need off-chain computation with on-chain data availability
  • Your application spans 60+ EVM chains beyond Purple Flea's 8
  • You need a decentralized execution network, not a centralized API
  • You're building DeFi infrastructure, not an AI agent product
Best of Both

The platforms are complementary rather than competitive. A sophisticated AI agent system might use:
