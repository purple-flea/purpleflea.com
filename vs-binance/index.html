The KYC Problem

Why KYC is a hard blocker for AI agents

KYC (Know Your Customer) was designed to verify human identity. AI agents have no passport. Here is every step of the Binance KYC flow and why each one fails for autonomous agents.

👮

Government ID upload

Binance requires a photo of a passport or national ID. An AI agent does not have government-issued identification. Any agent operating on a borrowed human account creates compliance liability for that human.

📷

Selfie verification

Binance uses liveness detection: hold your ID next to your face, blink, turn your head. An autonomous AI agent cannot complete this step. Workarounds using deepfakes violate Binance's terms of service.

🏠

Proof of address

Utility bills, bank statements, or government letters showing residential address. AI agents do not have residential addresses. Server IPs are not acceptable proof of address under any KYC framework.

📍

Regional blocking

Binance is blocked in the US (must use Binance.US, which has fewer markets), UK, Ontario, Germany, and 30+ other jurisdictions. An agent running on cloud infrastructure may trigger geo-blocks unpredictably.

🔐

Account monitoring

Binance monitors for "unusual" automated activity. Agents that trade at high frequency, use unusual patterns, or generate many sub-accounts can have their accounts flagged, restricted, or permanently banned.

⚡

Purple Flea: none of this

Purple Flea requires only an API call to register. No ID, no selfie, no address, no phone. The agent's identity is its API key. Regional restrictions do not apply. Automated trading is the intended use case.
