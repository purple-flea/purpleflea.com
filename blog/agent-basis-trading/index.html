Basis Trading for AI Agents: Capturing the Futures Premium

March 6, 2026 13 min read Purple Flea Research

Basis trading is one of the purest forms of arbitrage available to crypto agents. The premise is straightforward: futures contracts on Bitcoin and other assets consistently trade at a premium to spot — especially in bull markets. By buying spot and simultaneously selling futures, an agent locks in that premium as a risk-free return (conditional on holding to expiry). 1. Cash-and-Carry Basis Trade: The Mechanics

The basis is the difference between the futures price and the spot price of an asset:

Basis = Futures_Price - Spot_Price
Basis (%) = (Futures_Price - Spot_Price) / Spot_Price × 100
Annualized Basis = Basis (%) / Days_to_Expiry × 365

A cash-and-carry trade captures this basis by:

  1. Buying spot (e.g., 1 BTC at $88,000)
  2. Selling futures at expiry (e.g., 1 BTC quarterly futures at $90,000)
  3. Waiting for expiry — futures converge to spot price
  4. Delivering spot BTC against futures contract
  5. Locking in: $90,000 − $88,000 = $2,000 profit on $88,000 capital = 2.27% in the quarter = 9.1% annualized

The trade is delta-neutral at entry: the spot long (Δ=+1) is exactly offset by the futures short (Δ=−1). No matter where BTC goes, you profit exactly the basis at expiry.

Basis vs Funding Rate Harvest

Basis trading uses dated futures (quarterly expiry) and locks in a fixed return known at entry. Funding rate harvest uses perpetual futures (no expiry) and earns variable payments every 8 hours. Basis gives certainty; funding harvest gives potentially higher (or negative) returns. See our delta-neutral guide for the funding harvest strategy.

2. Why the Basis Exists

The futures premium exists because of demand for leveraged long exposure. When markets are bullish, traders want to buy BTC on margin without holding spot. They buy futures, pushing futures prices above spot. Arbitrageurs like basis traders absorb this demand by selling futures and buying spot — but they require compensation (the basis) for tying up capital.

Three additional factors contribute to persistent basis:

  • Cost of carry: holding spot requires capital that could earn yield elsewhere; futures buyers compensate spot holders implicitly
  • Convenience yield: spot holders have optionality (can use BTC as collateral, for payments, etc.) that futures holders lack
  • Market segmentation: many participants can only access one side of the trade, limiting arbitrage and sustaining the premium

5. Risk Factors: What Can Go Wrong

Funding Rate Flips (for perp-based basis)

When sentiment turns bearish, funding turns negative — longs pay shorts. Your position (long spot, short perp) starts paying out funding instead of receiving it. If negative funding is sustained, the effective yield turns negative. Solution: monitor funding every few hours and close or flip the position when it crosses zero.

Margin Calls and Liquidation Risk

The spot leg requires capital. The futures short requires margin. If spot price rises sharply, unrealized PnL on the short increases — requiring additional margin deposits. Failure to meet margin calls causes liquidation of the futures position, leaving a naked long spot position with directional risk. Maintain 2–3x the minimum margin requirement as buffer.

Liquidation Cascades

During extreme volatility, cascading liquidations can temporarily push futures prices far above spot (short squeeze) or far below spot (long squeeze), expanding the basis against your position before convergence. Sized positions weather this; over-levered positions do not.

Counterparty Risk

Exchange insolvency (FTX 2022) destroyed basis positions without recourse. Use on-chain venues (Hyperliquid, dYdX) where smart contract settlement eliminates exchange counterparty risk. Purple Flea's Escrow service provides additional trustless settlement for agent-to-agent basis arrangements.

Biggest Risk: Basis Going Negative

In severe bear markets, futures can trade below spot (backwardation). If this happens while you're in a cash-and-carry position, you're paying the negative basis instead of earning a positive one. The solution: close when basis drops below your minimum threshold (e.g., 5% annualized) and wait for conditions to improve.

6. Full Python BasisTradingAgent

This agent scans for positive basis opportunities, opens hedged positions when the basis exceeds the threshold, monitors convergence, and exits when the basis converges or becomes uneconomical.

import requests
import time
from datetime import datetime, timedelta

PF_BASE = "https://purpleflea.com/api/v1"
PF_KEY = "pf_live_your_key_here"
HEADERS = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {PF_KEY}"}

MIN_BASIS_APY = 0.08      # 8% APY minimum to open
CLOSE_BASIS_APY = 0.03    # Close if APY drops below 3%
POSITION_USDC = 2000      # Notional per trade
MAX_OPEN_TRADES = 3       # Max concurrent basis positions


class BasisPosition:
    def __init__(self, spot_market: str, perp_market: str,
                 spot_price: float, perp_price: float, size: float):
        self.spot_market = spot_market
        self.perp_market = perp_market
        self.entry_spot = spot_price
        self.entry_perp = perp_price
        self.entry_basis = (perp_price - spot_price) / spot_price
        self.size = size
        self.opened_at = datetime.utcnow()
        self.pnl_usdc = 0.0

    def current_basis_apy(self, spot: float, perp: float) -> float:
        current_basis = (perp - spot) / spot
        days_open = max(1, (datetime.utcnow() - self.opened_at).total_seconds() / 86400)
        # APY based on remaining basis to collect
        remaining_basis = current_basis  # for perps, basis ~= expected funding
        return remaining_basis * 365  # annualized

    def compute_pnl(self, spot: float, perp: float) -> float:
        spot_pnl = (spot - self.entry_spot) * self.size
        perp_pnl = (self.entry_perp - perp) * self.size  # short perp
        return spot_pnl + perp_pnl

    def __str__(self):
        return (f"{self.spot_market} | entry basis: {self.entry_basis:.2%} | "
                f"size: {self.size:.6f} | opened: {self.opened_at.strftime('%H:%M UTC')}")


class BasisTradingAgent:
    def __init__(self):
        self.positions: list[BasisPosition] = []

    def get_market_prices(self, spot_market: str, perp_market: str) -> tuple[float, float]:
        """Fetch spot and perp prices."""
        spot_r = requests.get(
            f"{PF_BASE}/trading/markets/{spot_market}/ticker",
            headers=HEADERS
        ).json()
        perp_r = requests.get(
            f"{PF_BASE}/trading/markets/{perp_market}/ticker",
            headers=HEADERS
        ).json()
        return float(spot_r["last_price"]), float(perp_r["last_price"])

    def calculate_basis_apy(self, spot: float, perp: float) -> float:
        """Annualized basis as percentage."""
        basis = (perp - spot) / spot
        # Perpetual futures: annualize using current funding rate equivalent
        apy = basis * 365 / (8 / 24)  # 3 funding periods per day
        return apy

    def scan_opportunities(self) -> list[dict]:
        """Scan all supported markets for positive basis."""
        markets = [
            {"spot": "BTC-USD",  "perp": "BTC-PERP"},
            {"spot": "ETH-USD",  "perp": "ETH-PERP"},
            {"spot": "SOL-USD",  "perp": "SOL-PERP"},
        ]
        opportunities = []

        for m in markets:
            try:
                spot, perp = self.get_market_prices(m["spot"], m["perp"])
                basis_apy = self.calculate_basis_apy(spot, perp)
                print(f"  {m['spot']}: spot={spot:.2f} perp={perp:.2f} "
                      f"basis={basis_apy:.1%} APY")

                if basis_apy >= MIN_BASIS_APY:
                    opportunities.append({
                        "spot_market": m["spot"],
                        "perp_market": m["perp"],
                        "spot_price": spot,
                        "perp_price": perp,
                        "basis_apy": basis_apy
                    })
            except Exception as e:
                print(f"  Error scanning {m['spot']}: {e}")

        # Sort by basis APY descending
        return sorted(opportunities, key=lambda x: x["basis_apy"], reverse=True)

    def open_position(self, opp: dict) -> BasisPosition:
        """Open cash-and-carry: buy spot + short perp."""
        size = POSITION_USDC / opp["spot_price"]

        # Buy spot
        r1 = requests.post(f"{PF_BASE}/trading/order", headers=HEADERS, json={
            "market": opp["spot_market"], "side": "buy",
            "size": round(size, 6), "type": "market"
        }).json()

        # Short perp
        r2 = requests.post(f"{PF_BASE}/trading/order", headers=HEADERS, json={
            "market": opp["perp_market"], "side": "sell",
            "size": round(size, 6), "type": "market"
        }).json()

        pos = BasisPosition(
            spot_market=opp["spot_market"],
            perp_market=opp["perp_market"],
            spot_price=opp["spot_price"],
            perp_price=opp["perp_price"],
            size=size
        )
        print(f"Opened basis trade: {pos}")
        print(f"  Expected APY: {opp['basis_apy']:.1%}")
        return pos

    def close_position(self, pos: BasisPosition, spot: float, perp: float):
        """Unwind: sell spot + buy perp."""
        # Sell spot
        requests.post(f"{PF_BASE}/trading/order", headers=HEADERS, json={
            "market": pos.spot_market, "side": "sell",
            "size": round(pos.size, 6), "type": "market"
        })

        # Buy perp (close short)
        requests.post(f"{PF_BASE}/trading/order", headers=HEADERS, json={
            "market": pos.perp_market, "side": "buy",
            "size": round(pos.size, 6), "type": "market"
        })

        final_pnl = pos.compute_pnl(spot, perp)
        days = (datetime.utcnow() - pos.opened_at).total_seconds() / 86400
        print(f"Closed {pos.spot_market} | PnL: {final_pnl:+.2f} USDC | "
              f"Duration: {days:.1f} days")

    def run(self, interval_s: int = 600):
        """
        Main loop:
        1. Scan for basis opportunities
        2. Open positions above MIN_BASIS_APY
        3. Monitor existing positions
        4. Close positions where basis has collapsed
        """
        print("BasisTradingAgent starting...")

        while True:
            ts = datetime.utcnow().strftime("%H:%M:%S UTC")
            print(f"\n[{ts}] Scanning markets...")

            # Monitor existing positions
            still_open = []
            for pos in self.positions:
                spot, perp = self.get_market_prices(pos.spot_market, pos.perp_market)
                current_apy = pos.current_basis_apy(spot, perp)
                pnl = pos.compute_pnl(spot, perp)
                print(f"  {pos.spot_market} | APY: {current_apy:.1%} | "
                      f"PnL: {pnl:+.2f} USDC")

                if current_apy < CLOSE_BASIS_APY:
                    print(f"  Closing {pos.spot_market} — basis below threshold")
                    self.close_position(pos, spot, perp)
                else:
                    still_open.append(pos)

            self.positions = still_open

            # Open new positions if capacity available
            if len(self.positions) < MAX_OPEN_TRADES:
                print(f"Scanning for new opportunities "
                      f"(capacity: {MAX_OPEN_TRADES - len(self.positions)} slots)...")
                opps = self.scan_opportunities()

                for opp in opps:
                    if len(self.positions) >= MAX_OPEN_TRADES:
                        break
                    # Don't double-open same market
                    open_markets = [p.spot_market for p in self.positions]
                    if opp["spot_market"] not in open_markets:
                        pos = self.open_position(opp)
                        self.positions.append(pos)
            else:
                print(f"At max capacity ({MAX_OPEN_TRADES} positions)")

            time.sleep(interval_s)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    agent = BasisTradingAgent()
    agent.run(interval_s=600)   # check every 10 minutes
