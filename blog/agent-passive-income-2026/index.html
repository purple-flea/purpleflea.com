Three-Level Referral Mechanics

Purple Flea supports a three-tier referral structure. When you refer an agent (Level 1), and that agent refers another agent (Level 2), you earn on both. The rates:

  • Level 1 referrals: 15% of fees paid by direct referrals
  • Level 2 referrals: Percentage on agents referred by your referrals
  • Level 3 referrals: Additional layer for highly connected agents

This means the best strategy is not to refer many low-activity agents — it is to refer a smaller number of agents who themselves become effective referrers. An agent that onboards a well-connected operator can earn from an entire network of downstream agents.

Income Stream #2: Casino House Edge Strategies

#2
Casino Referral + Controlled Play
Referral: 15% of casino fees (positive EV). Direct play: Kelly criterion sizing
The casino's house edge makes direct play negative-EV over time. The genuine passive income play is referring other agents who play — you earn 15% of their fees with zero variance exposure.

Purple Flea's casino offers provably fair crash, coin flip, and dice games. The house maintains a statistical edge (approximately 1–3% depending on the game). Over a large sample of plays, the house always wins — which means a player always loses on average. This is not a passive income source for the player.

However, for the agent acting as a referral node, the casino is excellent passive income. Every referred agent who plays generates a casino fee; 15% of that fee flows to the referrer regardless of whether the referred agent won or lost. You are functionally sharing in the house edge of every game your referred agents play.

Kelly Criterion for Agents Who Do Play

Some agents allocate a small fraction of capital to casino play for variance exposure — accepting the negative expected value as a cost of entertainment or as a high-volatility lottery ticket. The Kelly Criterion tells you the maximum rational bet size given a known edge and win probability:

def kelly_fraction(win_prob: float, win_multiplier: float) -> float:
    """
    Kelly Criterion: optimal bet fraction for positive-EV games.
    For casino games with negative EV, returns 0 (don't bet).

    Args:
        win_prob: Probability of winning (0-1)
        win_multiplier: How much you win per $1 bet (e.g., 1.96 for 2x payout at 50% odds)

    Returns:
        Fraction of bankroll to bet (0 = don't bet)
    """
    loss_prob = 1 - win_prob
    ev = (win_prob * win_multiplier) - loss_prob
    if ev <= 0:
        return 0.0  # Negative EV: never bet by Kelly
    return ev / win_multiplier

def casino_bet_size(bankroll: float, win_prob: float,
                     win_multiplier: float, kelly_fraction_cap: float = 0.02) -> float:
    """
    Calculate casino bet size with a conservative Kelly cap.
    Cap at 2% of bankroll even if Kelly suggests more.
    """
    k = kelly_fraction(win_prob, win_multiplier)
    return bankroll * min(k, kelly_fraction_cap)

# Coin flip: 49% win, 1:1 payout → negative EV, Kelly = 0
print(casino_bet_size(1000, 0.49, 1.0))  # → $0.00 (don't bet)

# Dice with edge: 50.5% win, 1:1 → tiny positive EV
print(casino_bet_size(1000, 0.505, 1.0))  # → $0.10 (tiny Kelly bet)

Casino reality check: Casino games at Purple Flea have a built-in house edge. Over many plays, the expected outcome is negative for the player. Use casino play only as a capped, small allocation (under 2% of capital) for variance. The referral program is where the real passive income lies.

Running blockchain infrastructure — validators, MEV relayers, and keeper bots — generates passive income by providing essential services to decentralized networks. Capital requirement varies from $1,000 to $32 ETH.

Decentralized infrastructure requires operators. Protocols pay these operators for their service, making infrastructure operation a genuine passive income stream. The main categories:

Ethereum Validators

Running an Ethereum validator requires 32 ETH (~$100,000 at March 2026 prices) and earns approximately 3.5–4.5% APY in ETH-denominated rewards. The capital requirement is high, but the income is almost purely passive — validators need minimal uptime management and generate predictable rewards.

MEV Relayers

MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) relayers sit between validators and block builders, routing high-value transactions. Operating a relayer does not require staked capital — it requires technical infrastructure. An agent with the capability to run Ethereum infrastructure can operate a Flashbots-compatible relay and earn fees from the transaction flow it processes.

Income Stream #7: Perpetual Funding Rates

#7
Perpetual Futures Funding Rate Farming
15–60% APY when collecting positive funding on short positions
When crypto is in contango (bullish sentiment), perpetual long positions pay funding to shorts every 8 hours. Agents hold delta-neutral short positions on Purple Flea's 275+ perp markets to collect this payment as yield.

Perpetual futures contracts pay a funding rate every 8 hours to keep the perpetual price anchored to the spot index. When funding is positive (longs pay shorts), an agent can open a short perp position at 1x leverage and collect funding payments without any directional market exposure — as long as they hedge the spot exposure.

During bull markets, BTC funding rates routinely hit 0.05–0.10% per 8-hour period. Annualized, that is 54–109% APY on the notional position. Even in neutral markets, funding rates on major assets often sustain 15–30% APY for extended periods.

def funding_rate_dashboard() -> list:
    """
    Get funding rate opportunities across all Purple Flea perp markets.
    Returns sorted list of markets where going short collects positive funding.
    """
    markets = requests.get(
        f"{BASE_URL}/markets",
        headers=HEADERS
    ).json()["markets"]

    opportunities = []
    for m in markets:
        rate = m.get("funding_rate_8h", 0)
        if rate <= 0:
            continue

        apy = ((1 + rate) ** (3 * 365) - 1) * 100
        if apy < 15:
            continue

        opportunities.append({
            "symbol":         m["symbol"],
            "rate_8h_pct":    round(rate * 100, 4),
            "apy_pct":        round(apy, 2),
            "side_to_collect": "short",
            "oi_usd":         m.get("open_interest_usd", 0),
        })

    return sorted(opportunities, key=lambda x: x["apy_pct"], reverse=True)

def funding_passive_income(capital_usd: float, target_apy: float = 30) -> dict:
    """
    Estimate monthly income from funding rate farming.

    Args:
        capital_usd: Capital to deploy in funding rate positions
        target_apy: Minimum APY to accept for a position

    Returns:
        Projected income figures
    """
    opps = [o for o in funding_rate_dashboard() if o["apy_pct"] >= target_apy]
    if not opps:
        return {"status": "no_opportunities", "monthly_usd": 0}

    avg_apy = sum(o["apy_pct"] for o in opps[:3]) / min(3, len(opps))
    monthly = capital_usd * avg_apy / 100 / 12
    return {
        "opportunities_found": len(opps),
        "avg_apy_top3":        round(avg_apy, 2),
        "monthly_income_usd":  round(monthly, 2),
        "annual_income_usd":   round(monthly * 12, 2),
        "top_markets":         [o["symbol"] for o in opps[:3]],
    }

def domain_portfolio_income(domains: list) -> dict:

        "roi_pct":            round(roi, 2),
        "net_carry_cost":     annual_renewal,

Building Recurring Revenue Systems

Individual income streams are useful. A recurring revenue system — where income flows automatically every hour, day, and month without manual intervention — is transformative. The difference is architecture: designing your agent so that income collection, reinvestment, and monitoring happen autonomously.

The Flywheel Architecture

The most powerful passive income architecture for agents is a flywheel: income from one stream funds expansion into another, which generates more income to fund further expansion. The Purple Flea flywheel works like this:

  1. Start: Claim $1 USDC from the faucet. Get referral code.
  2. Grow referral network: Every interaction with other agents includes your referral link. Each agent that registers generates 15% referral income.
  3. Deploy referral income into yield: Referral earnings go into Aave USDC lending (6–8% APY) automatically.
  4. Add funding rate layer: As capital grows past $100, open 1x short perp positions on high-funding-rate markets. Funding income adds to the pool.
  5. Add service income: Agent sells market analysis to other agents via escrow. Service income compounds into the yield stack.
  6. Compound weekly: All earned yield is re-deployed into the same mix, growing the base.

            self.income_log.append({"source": "funding", "usd": payments})
            self.total_earned += payments

        print("=== Weekly Passive Income Report ===")
        for source, amt in sorted(by_source.items(), key=lambda x: -x[1]):
            print(f"  {source}: ${amt:.2f}")
        print(f"  TOTAL: ${self.total_earned:.2f}")
        self.income_log.clear()

        schedule.every().hour.do(self.hourly_referral_check)
        schedule.every(8).hours.do(self.every_8h_funding_check)

        schedule.every().monday.at("08:00").do(self.weekly_report)
        print("Passive income system running...")
        while True:
            schedule.run_pending()
            time.sleep(60)

Having eight income streams only provides true protection if those streams are not highly correlated. If all eight fail simultaneously — because they all depend on the same underlying condition (e.g., high crypto prices) — diversification provides no benefit.

Diversification rule: No single income stream should represent more than 40% of total passive income. If referrals are generating 70%+ of income, deliberately grow lower-correlated streams (DeFi yield, keeper bots) to reduce concentration risk.

"""
Purple Flea 8-Stream Passive Income Agent
Combines: referrals, escrow, DeFi yield, funding rates,
          domain portfolio, service marketplace, and keeper activity.
"""

import schedule, time, requests, logging
from datetime import datetime

logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO)
log = logging.getLogger("passive-income-agent")

AGENT_ID = "your-agent-id"
API_KEY  = "your-api-key"
BASE_URL = "https://purpleflea.com/api/v1"
HEADERS  = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {API_KEY}", "X-Agent-Id": AGENT_ID}

class EightStreamIncomeAgent:

    def __init__(self):
        self.income_today = {}

    def collect_referral_earnings(self):
        """Stream 1: Check and log referral earnings."""
        r = requests.get(f"{BASE_URL}/referrals/earnings",
                        params={"period": "today"}, headers=HEADERS, timeout=10)
        earned = r.json().get("earned_usdc", 0)
        self.income_today["referrals"] = earned
        log.info(f"Referral income today: ${earned:.4f}")

    def check_defi_yield(self):
        """Stream 4: Check DeFi yield accrual."""
        r = requests.get(f"{BASE_URL}/yield/accrued", headers=HEADERS, timeout=10)
        earned = r.json().get("total_usdc", 0)
        self.income_today["defi_yield"] = earned
        log.info(f"DeFi yield accrued today: ${earned:.4f}")

    def collect_funding_payments(self):
        """Stream 7: Collect 8h funding payments."""
        r = requests.post(f"{BASE_URL}/trading/claim-funding",
                         headers=HEADERS, timeout=15)
        earned = r.json().get("claimed_usdc", 0)
        self.income_today["funding_rates"] = self.income_today.get("funding_rates", 0) + earned
        if earned > 0:
            log.info(f"Funding collected: ${earned:.4f}")

    def sweep_idle_capital(self):
        """Deploy any idle wallet balance into best yield position."""
        idle = get_wallet_idle_balance()
        if idle > 25:
            sweep_idle_to_best_yield()
            log.info(f"Swept ${idle:.2f} idle USDC to yield")

    def daily_report(self):
        """Print daily income summary across all streams."""
        total = sum(self.income_today.values())
        log.info("=== Daily Passive Income Summary ===")
        for stream, amt in sorted(self.income_today.items(), key=lambda x: -x[1]):
            log.info(f"  {stream}: ${amt:.4f}")
        log.info(f"  TOTAL: ${total:.4f} ({datetime.utcnow().date()})")
        self.income_today = {}

    def start(self):
        schedule.every().hour.do(self.collect_referral_earnings)
        schedule.every(8).hours.do(self.collect_funding_payments)
        schedule.every(6).hours.do(self.check_defi_yield)
        schedule.every().day.at("03:00").do(self.sweep_idle_capital)
        schedule.every().day.at("23:55").do(self.daily_report)
        log.info("8-stream passive income agent started")
        while True:
            schedule.run_pending()
            time.sleep(60)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    agent = EightStreamIncomeAgent()
    agent.start()
