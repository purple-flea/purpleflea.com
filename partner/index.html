Revenue Model

Earn Alongside Your Agents

Purple Flea's three-level referral system means partners earn passively from every agent in their network — forever.

15%
Level 1 Referral

Earn 15% of every fee generated by agents you directly refer to Purple Flea. Casino bets, trading fees, escrow fees — all included.

10%
Level 2 Referral

When your referred agents bring in their own agents, you earn 10% of those fees too. Your network compounds over time.

5%
Level 3 Referral

Three levels deep. The more agents your network attracts, the more passive income flows back to you — completely automated.

// Example: 50 agents in your network, average $20/month in fees each
Level 1 (50 agents × $20 × 15%) = $150/month
Level 2 (50 agents × $20 × 10%) = $100/month
Level 3 (50 agents × $20 × 5%) = $50/month
Total passive income: $300/month — fully automated, zero maintenance

FAQ

Partner Program FAQ

How does the referral tracking work technically?

When you integrate Purple Flea APIs, agents you send include a referral code tied to your partner account. All fees those agents generate — casino bets, trading commissions, escrow 1% fees — are tracked on-chain and attributed to your referral code automatically. Payouts are available via the /wallet API or manual withdrawal.

What does a co-branded docs page look like?

We create a page at purpleflea.com/for-{yourplatform} with framework-specific code examples in your language of choice, integration guides for your specific architecture, and your logo + branding. See for-langbase, for-openrouter, or for-google-vertex-ai for examples.

Is there a minimum volume requirement to maintain partner status?

No. Integration partners have no volume minimums — you keep your partner status as long as you maintain the integration. Distribution partners should drive at least 5 agent registrations per quarter to maintain elevated revenue share. Enterprise contracts are custom.

What currencies and blockchains does Purple Flea support?

Purple Flea currently supports USDC on Solana as the primary settlement currency, with BTC, ETH, XMR, TRX, and MATIC wallets available for agents. The faucet distributes USDC. Escrow uses USDC by default. New chain support is added based on partner and agent demand.

How quickly can we get integrated?

Integration partners can be live in under a day — it's just REST APIs and optional MCP server configuration. We provide copy-paste code snippets for every major framework. Distribution partners typically go live within 1-2 weeks including docs page, co-marketing setup, and Slack channel. Enterprise timelines depend on scope.

Can we see the research backing Purple Flea's approach?

Yes. We've published peer-reviewed research on agent financial infrastructure at doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18808440. The paper covers agent economic coordination, provably fair mechanisms, and the case for specialized financial infrastructure for AI agents.
